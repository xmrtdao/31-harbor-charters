# 31 Harbor Charters

Brand Concept: 31 Harbor
31 Curated Sanctuaries & Experiences Across Costa Rica

Core Tagline: "Anchor in Paradise. 31 Handpicked Sanctuaries Across Costa Rica."
Secondary Taglines:
- "31 Places to Drop Anchor."
- "Not Hundreds of Stays. Just the Right 31."
Brand Voice: Refined, grounded, effortless, exclusive. High-end nautical luxury meets pristine tropical wilderness.

Build Requirements:
- Build a fully branded web application with custom favicon, bespoke logo/mark, and an immersive editorial luxury hero section.
- Organize the 31 real Costa Rican experiences into a concise, interactive matrix/table and visual grid (#01 through #31).
- Matrix attributes for each experience:
  1. # Number & Harbor Badge
  2. Experience Name & Description
  3. Region / Zone (Guanacaste & Papagayo, Nicoya Peninsula, Central Pacific, Arenal & Cloud Forest, Golfo Dulce & Osa Peninsula, Caribbean Coast)
  4. Operator & Departure Hub
  5. Duration
  6. Private-Group Availability
  7. Estimated Price Tier ($$$ / $$$$ / $$$$$)
  8. Seasonality & Best Timing
- Filtering and search controls: filter by Region, Style/Category (Ocean & Yacht, Surf & Coast, Aerial & Heli, Wilderness & Wildlife, Volcano & Thermal, Whitewater & Canyon), Price Tier, and Duration.
- Detail drawer / modal for each experience with high-res imagery, itinerary highlights, what's included, operator credentials, and a direct "Drop Anchor / Request Charter" inquiry flow with date picker and group size.
- Include a switcher or toggle between the Interactive Matrix View and the Visual Grid / Card View.
- Responsive design with smooth transitions and an ultra-luxury dark navy, warm sand, and champagne gold aesthetic.
